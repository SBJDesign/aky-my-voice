Privacy notice

Last updated

This notice explains what information the My Voice, My Future website collects, why, who can see it, and how you can have it changed or removed. It follows the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Who we are

The site is run by , in support of Governor Abba Kabir Yusuf. You can reach us about your data at or .

What we collect

  • Your full name, local government area and ward
  • Your phone number, and your email address if you give it
  • The priority you choose and the message you write or pick
  • A record of what you agreed to, with the date and time
Your photo is never uploaded. It stays on your phone and is only used there to draw your card.

Why we use it

  • To make your card
  • To count voices by LGA, ward and priority, and to understand what matters to people in each area
  • To send you campaign updates by SMS, WhatsApp, call or email, because you agreed to this
  • To show your first name, LGA and message in Voices of Kano, only if you chose "Feature my message" and our team approved it

We rely on your consent. You can withdraw it at any time, and doing so does not affect anything done before you withdrew.

Who can see it

Only a small number of authorised campaign administrators can see names with phone numbers and emails. Anyone visiting the site can see total counts, and approved messages with first name and LGA only. Your phone number and email are never shown publicly.

We do not sell your information or give it to anyone outside the campaign. It is stored on secure servers run by our hosting provider, Supabase, which processes it only on our behalf.

How long we keep it

We keep your information until , and then delete it. If you ask us to delete it sooner, we will.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • Tell you what information we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete your information
  • Stop contacting you

Contact us at or with the phone number you used, and we will act within 30 days. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

One entry per phone number

Each phone number is counted once. If the same number is used again, a new card is made, but it is not counted a second time.
